Privacy policy

Pod People Scheduler. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Who this applies to

Pod People Scheduler is a private tool used only by the Pod People podcast to manage its own YouTube channel and its own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and the only Google and Instagram accounts connected to it belong to the show.

What it accesses

With the YouTube access the show grants it (the YouTube Data API scope "Manage your YouTube account"), the tool:

  • uploads the show's own videos and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, publish times and visibility;
  • adds those videos to the show's own playlists;
  • reads the list of videos on the show's channel, so it doesn't upload an episode twice.

It does not read comments, messages, subscribers' details, or any other YouTube or Google data, and it never deletes videos. For Instagram it publishes the show's own posts and a first comment, and reads the show's recent posts.

How data is stored and shared

The sign-in tokens and the posting schedule are stored only on the show's own computer, in a file readable only by its user account. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram) to carry out the uploads and posts the show asks for. The tool has no server, no analytics, no advertising, and doesn't sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Pod People Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can remove the tool's access at any time from Google account permissions, and from the Instagram app's settings. Deleting the tool's folder on the computer removes everything it stored.
