Pod People Scheduler

A private tool for the Pod People podcast

Pod People Scheduler is a small program the hosts of Pod People, a weekly horror movie podcast, run on their own computer. It uploads and schedules episodes and Shorts on the show's own YouTube channel, and publishes posts to the show's own Instagram account, @podpeoplehorror.

It isn't a public service and it doesn't sign anyone else in. Only the show's own accounts are connected to it.
